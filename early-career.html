Origins / 1997—2015

BUILT
EARLY.

From a karting family in Hasselt to the youngest Formula 1 starter in history, Max Verstappen's rise was fast—but never accidental.

1997

A racing family

Born in Hasselt, Belgium, Max grew up immersed in motorsport. His father, Jos Verstappen, raced in Formula 1; his mother, Sophie Kumpen, was an accomplished karter.

2005

Karting begins

At age seven, Max started karting. The instinct for racing and a fierce competitiveness appeared immediately.

2013

World champion

He won the KZ World Championship in karting, adding to a trophy cabinet built across European and international competition.

2014

Single-seater breakthrough

One standout Formula 3 season brought six consecutive wins at the Zandvoort Masters and placed him firmly on Formula 1's radar.

2015

Formula 1 debut

At 17 years and 166 days old, Verstappen became Formula 1's youngest race starter, beginning a new era with Toro Rosso.
